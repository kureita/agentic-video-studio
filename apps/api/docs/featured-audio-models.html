Overview

Runware's audio platform spans three categories across 4 providers — ElevenLabs, MiniMax, KlingAI, and Runware (Ovi). For a video studio, all three are essential:

  • Music Text-to-Music — Generate instrumental or vocal tracks from text prompts. Background scores, jingles, and soundtracks.
  • SFX Sound Effects & Sound Design — Generate or synchronize SFX, ambient audio, and foley from video or text. Critical for post-production.
  • TTS Text-to-Speech — Convert text to natural spoken audio. Voiceovers, narration, dialogue, and accessibility.

This document recommends 9 featured models — 1 music, 3 SFX/sound design (including 1 coming soon), and 5 TTS models. The remaining 4 are legacy ElevenLabs models noted for completeness.

Audio pricing note: TTS is priced per 1,000 characters or per-character credits. Music is priced per minute of generated audio. SFX/sound design models use per-generation or per-second pricing (check Playground for exact costs). Some ElevenLabs TTS models don't show explicit pricing on Runware — they use character-based billing visible in the Playground.
