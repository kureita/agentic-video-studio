02

Creative Concept

A lone Nike Pegasus sitting on a sidewalk gets tired of waiting for its owner. It starts tapping impatiently, gets up, and sprints through the city on its own. Crosses a finish line. Gets a medal. The AI-generated physics are intentionally imperfect — the jank is the joke. One punchline: your shoes are more disciplined than you.

Shot List — Beat Breakdown

  • 0:00 – 0:01 Hook. Shoe alone on sidewalk. Text overlay sets the premise. Quiet, cinematic.
  • 0:01 – 0:03 Shoe taps impatiently. Lace rises like a tentacle. Tension builds. Comedic score fades in.
  • 0:03 – 0:06 Shoe lifts itself up and starts running. Wobbly, ridiculous form. Tracking shot follows it down the street.
  • 0:06 – 0:10 Full sprint through a crowd. Pedestrians confused. Speed ramps. Flash frames. Peak energy.
  • 0:10 – 0:13 Finish line. Tape breaks. Confetti. Medal drops. Hero moment. Absurdly triumphant.
  • 0:13 – 0:15 End card. Tagline fades in. Nike Pegasus. Swoosh. Out.

Image Nodes — Style Frames

Style frame references generated before video production. These anchor visual consistency across scenes and are passed as image references into downstream video nodes.

Style Frame Generation

IMG-01 Shoe Hero Reference Image
Aspect: 9:16 Resolution: 1080x1920 Style: Commercial Product Photography
Generation Prompt
Product photography, Nike Pegasus running shoe, matte gray and volt neon green colorway, three-quarter angle, sitting upright on raw concrete surface. Harsh directional sunlight from upper left, sharp cast shadow. Shallow depth of field, background falls to smooth bokeh. Clean minimal composition, no props, no people. Commercial editorial style, high detail, photorealistic. Vertical 9:16 frame.

This is the hero asset. Every video node featuring the shoe should maintain visual consistency with this reference — colorway, angle language, and surface texture.

IMG-02 Urban Street Environment Image
Aspect: 9:16 Resolution: 1080x1920 Style: Cinematic Street Photography
Generation Prompt
Empty city sidewalk, early morning golden hour. Wet concrete pavement with subtle reflections. Blurred pedestrians and storefronts in the background at f/1.8 depth of field. Desaturated cinematic color grading, lifted blacks, warm highlight tones. Wide establishing composition, low angle close to ground level. Urban street photography. No text, no logos. Vertical 9:16 frame.

Environment reference shared by VID-01, VID-03, and VID-04. The low angle and wet pavement sell the "shoe-level" perspective.

IMG-03 Finish Line Environment Image
Aspect: 9:16 Resolution: 1080x1920 Style: Cinematic Sports Photography
Generation Prompt
Marathon finish line, red and white tape stretched across an empty city road. Morning light, golden hour flare from behind. Confetti frozen mid-air. Crowd silhouettes in soft focus background, out of focus bokeh lights. Low angle hero shot looking slightly upward. Cinematic sports photography, triumphant atmosphere. Desaturated color palette with warm highlights. No people in foreground. Vertical 9:16 frame.
IMG-04 End Card Layout Image
Aspect: 9:16 Resolution: 1080x1920 Style: Graphic Design / Typography
Generation Prompt
Minimal graphic design layout on solid black background. Clean white sans-serif typography centered in frame. Upper text area at 40% height, logo area at 70% height. Pure black and white only, no color, no gradients. Editorial typography poster style. High contrast. Vertical 9:16 frame.

Used as spatial reference only. Actual text and swoosh logo are composited by the editor node in Remotion with proper font rendering and animation.

05

Video Nodes — Scene Generation

Each node generates a single scene clip. Reference images from Section 04 are fed in as style/subject anchors. Clips are generated slightly longer than their final timeline duration to allow trimming flexibility in the editor.

Scene Clips

VID-01 Hook — Shoe on Sidewalk Video
Timeline: 0:00 – 0:01 Gen Duration: 3s (trim to 1s) Camera: Slow push-in
References: IMG-01 IMG-02
Generation Prompt
A single Nike running shoe sitting upright, alone on a concrete city sidewalk. Early morning golden hour light. Slow cinematic push-in camera move toward the shoe. Shallow depth of field, urban environment softly blurred behind. The shoe is completely still. No people visible, no movement except camera. Quiet, contemplative mood. Commercial film quality, desaturated color grading, lifted blacks. 9:16 vertical, 3 seconds.

Generate at 3 seconds. The editor will use the best 1-second segment where the framing is tightest. The stillness here is critical — it makes the movement in VID-02 hit harder.

VID-02 Impatient Tap Video
Timeline: 0:01 – 0:03 Gen Duration: 4s (trim to 2s) Camera: Static, low angle, slight zoom
References: IMG-01
Generation Prompt
Close-up of a Nike running shoe on concrete. The shoe begins tapping its toe impatiently, as if a foot is inside but there is no foot, the shoe moves on its own. The shoelace slowly rises upward and sways like a living tentacle, searching the air. Slightly unnatural physics, the movement is organic but wrong. Low angle shot, shallow depth of field. Cinematic lighting, morning sun. The motion starts subtle and becomes increasingly agitated. Eerie yet comedic tone. 9:16 vertical, 4 seconds.

The lace movement is the key moment. AI physics will make it look slightly alien — do not attempt to correct this. If the generation produces extra visual artifacts on the shoe's surface, regenerate only if the shoe silhouette is unrecognizable.

VID-03 Shoe Gets Up and Runs Video
Timeline: 0:03 – 0:06 Gen Duration: 5s (trim to 3s) Camera: Side tracking shot
References: IMG-01 IMG-02
Generation Prompt
A single Nike running shoe lifts itself off the ground and begins running down a city sidewalk entirely on its own, no foot inside. The shoe wobbles, stumbles, and has terrible running form, bouncing unevenly and flopping side to side. Comedic and absurd motion. Side-angle tracking shot following the shoe at shoe-level as it moves left to right. Slight motion blur. Urban street with golden hour morning light, shallow depth of field on background. The shoe is determined despite looking ridiculous. Cinematic quality, desaturated tones. 9:16 vertical, 5 seconds.

This is the comedic core of the video. The wobble and bad form are essential — this is where AI-generated physics become a creative asset. Generate 3-4 variations and select the one with the most personality in the shoe's "gait." The tracking camera should keep the shoe roughly centered.

VID-04 Sprint Through Crowd Video
Timeline: 0:06 – 0:10 Gen Duration: 6s (trim to 4s) Camera: Dynamic low-angle tracking
References: IMG-01 IMG-02
Generation Prompt
A Nike running shoe sprinting at high speed down a busy city sidewalk by itself, weaving between the legs of pedestrians. The pedestrians look down confused and startled as the shoe races past. Dynamic low-angle tracking shot following the shoe, camera close to the ground. Heavy motion blur on background elements, shoe stays sharp. Action movie energy, fast and kinetic. The shoe dodges obstacles and cuts between people. Urban environment, warm golden light, cinematic film grain. The scene feels chaotic and exhilarating. 9:16 vertical, 6 seconds.

Peak energy scene. The editor will apply speed ramping (80% slow at entry, accelerating to 120%) and single-frame white flash interrupts at the cut points. Generate with slightly more pedestrians than needed — the density sells the chaos.

VID-05 Finish Line Victory Video
Timeline: 0:10 – 0:13 Gen Duration: 5s (trim to 3s) Camera: Low angle hero shot, slight slow-motion
References: IMG-01 IMG-03
Generation Prompt
A Nike running shoe breaking through a marathon finish line tape, crossing the line triumphantly by itself, no runner. Slow motion. Confetti explodes from above and falls around the shoe. A gold medal on a ribbon drops from the sky and lands on top of the shoe. The shoe stands perfectly still after crossing, as if posing victoriously. Low angle hero shot looking up at the shoe, dramatic backlighting with golden lens flare. Crowd silhouettes cheering in soft focus background. Epic cinematic sports photography feel, triumphant and absurd. 9:16 vertical, 5 seconds.

The medal landing is the punctuation mark. If AI fails to generate a convincing medal drop, the editor node will composite a medal graphic in Remotion with a physics-based drop animation as fallback.

VID-06 End Card Background Video
Timeline: 0:13 – 0:15 Gen Duration: 3s (trim to 2s) Camera: Static
References: IMG-04
Generation Prompt
Slow, subtle particle dust floating in a beam of light against a pure black background. Minimal, almost invisible movement. Tiny white and gold specks drifting downward very slowly. Cinematic, elegant, quiet. The particles are barely there, just enough to make the black feel alive. 9:16 vertical, 3 seconds.

This is a background texture layer only. All text, logo, and animations for the end card are handled by Remotion in the editor node. This just prevents a dead-flat black screen and adds subtle life behind the typography.

06

Audio Nodes

Four audio assets covering ambient sound, score, fanfare, and a branded sound effect. The editor handles all mixing, volume automation, and sync. Each node generates raw audio at the specified duration.

Audio Generation

AUD-01 City Ambient Audio
Duration: 15 seconds Role: Background bed Mix level: -12dB to -14dB
Generation Prompt
Quiet early morning city ambience. Distant traffic hum, no horns. Soft bird calls, occasional. Gentle urban atmosphere, realistic field recording style. Subtle and understated, designed to sit beneath music and sound effects without competing. Stereo, warm tone, no wind noise. 15 seconds, seamless loop-friendly.
AUD-02 Comedic Tension Score Audio
Duration: 12 seconds Role: Underscore Mix level: -8dB to -4dB (builds)
Generation Prompt
Orchestral comedic underscore. Starts with quiet plucked pizzicato strings creating a sense of mischief and curiosity. Playful woodwind accents join at 3 seconds. The energy builds progressively, adding snare rolls and brass stabs. By 8 seconds the piece is at full chaotic energy, fast-paced and kinetic, like an action comedy chase scene. Think the tone of a Wes Anderson film score crossed with a heist movie trailer. Whimsical but cinematic. Ends abruptly at 12 seconds on a hard stop, no fade. Orchestral instruments only, no synths. 12 seconds.

The hard stop at the end is intentional — it cuts to silence exactly when the finish line fanfare takes over. The editor aligns the score's peak energy with the sprint scene (VID-04).

AUD-03 Victory Fanfare Audio
Duration: 4 seconds Role: Punctuation / transition Mix level: -2dB
Generation Prompt
Short epic orchestral victory fanfare. Bright brass section playing a triumphant 4-note ascending melody. Timpani hit on the downbeat. Cymbal crash. The tone is heroic but slightly over-the-top, as if celebrating something absurdly small with disproportionate grandeur. Think Olympic medal ceremony compressed into 3 seconds. Punchy, loud, then fading to silence over the final second. Orchestral only. 4 seconds.
AUD-04 Swoosh Sound Effect Audio
Duration: 1.5 seconds Role: Logo sting Mix level: -3dB
Generation Prompt
Clean sharp whoosh cutting through air, left to right pan, followed by a deep subtle bass drop impact. Modern sound design, punchy and tight. The whoosh is fast, under half a second, the bass hit resonates for another second then fades. Cinematic trailer style. 1.5 seconds total.

Syncs to the Nike swoosh logo reveal on the end card. The editor triggers this at the exact frame the logo begins its scale-in animation.

Editor Agent — Remotion Directive

The complete prompt passed to the editor agent. This instructs the LLM-powered Remotion compositor on timeline assembly, text overlays, motion graphics, audio mixing, effects, and export. This is the final node in the pipeline — it consumes all upstream outputs.

GLOBAL POST-PROCESSING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Color grade Apply to entire timeline:
Saturation: 0.85 (15% desaturation)
Blacks lift: +8 (via curves — raise shadow floor)
Highlights warmth: shift +5 toward warm (#FFF8F0 tint at 3%)
Contrast: 1.05 (subtle increase)

Film grain Overlay noise texture at 2.5% opacity across all frames
Monochromatic grain, animated (different noise per frame)
Adds texture that masks AI smoothness

Vignette Subtle radial darkening
Radius: 85% of frame, feather: 40%
Opacity: 12%
Center: 50% 45% (slightly above center)

Safe zones Keep all text and critical action within:
Top: 10% margin (platform UI overlap)
Bottom: 15% margin (captions, engagement bar)
Sides: 5% margin

AUDIO MASTER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Bus routing Ambient (AUD-01) → Bus A → Master
Score (AUD-02) → Bus B → Master
SFX (AUD-03/04) → Bus C → Master

Master chain Limiter: ceiling -1.0dB, release 100ms
Stereo width: 90% (slight narrowing for phone speakers)
High-pass filter: 60Hz (remove sub-bass rumble)

Ducking When AUD-03 or AUD-04 play, duck AUD-01 by additional -6dB
Attack: 10ms, Release: 200ms
