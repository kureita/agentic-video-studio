Overview

Runware offers 69 image generation models across dozens of providers. This document narrows the selection to 13 featured models recommended for surfacing in the app, covering three tiers:

  • Premium — Flagship models from top providers with maximum quality and multiple configurations.
  • Mid-Range — Strong quality-to-cost ratio, suitable as defaults for most users.
  • Budget — Ultra-cheap or ultra-fast options for previews, prototyping, and high-volume batches.

Models were selected based on: provider brand recognition, output quality (per Runware's "Best Image" collection), number of configurable pricing tiers, and practical price range for end-users.

Configuration Mapping Notes

When mapping these models into the app, key dimensions to account for:

Pricing Dimensions
  • Resolution-based — Most models (Nano Banana 2, Kling O3, FLUX.2 dev). Price scales with output resolution.
  • Quality-tier-based — GPT Image 1.5 uniquely offers Low / Medium / High quality at each resolution. Three-dimensional pricing grid.
  • Megapixel formula — FLUX.2 [max] uses a per-megapixel formula with add-on costs for reference images.
  • Flat-rate — Seedream 5.0 Lite, Recraft V4, Recraft V4 Pro, FLUX.2 [flex] charge a single price regardless of resolution (up to a cap).
  • Add-ons — Some models charge extra for input images (editing) or grounded search features.
