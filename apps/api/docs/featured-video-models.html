Overview

Runware offers 68 video generation models across many providers. This document selects 14 featured models across three tiers:

  • Premium — Flagship models with maximum cinematic quality, native audio, and the most configurations.
  • Mid-Range — Strong quality-to-cost ratio, good defaults for most video generation use cases.
  • Budget — Fast or cheap options for drafts, previews, prototyping, and high-volume batches.
Video pricing note: Unlike image models (per-image), video models are priced per-second of output. Most configs combine resolution, duration, and optional audio as pricing dimensions. Some models quote per-clip (e.g. 6s or 8s), others per-second.

Configuration Mapping Notes

Video pricing is more complex than images due to multiple dimensions. Key patterns:

Pricing Dimensions
  • Resolution — Nearly all models scale price with resolution (720p → 1080p → 4K).
  • Duration — Per-second (LTX, Kling, Vidu) vs fixed-clip (Sora 8s, MiniMax 6/10s, Seedance 5s, PixVerse 5s, Grok 6s).
  • Audio toggle — Many models charge extra for native audio (typically 1.5×–2× the no-audio price).
  • Input type — Some models (Grok, Kling O3) vary price by T2V vs I2V vs V2V input mode.
  • Orientation — LTX 2.3 uniquely supports both landscape and portrait at the same price per resolution tier.
